Indian Heritage Centre Deepavali Open House 2026: House of Memories

The Indian Heritage Centre celebrates Deepavali with a free Open House, with storytelling, crafts and a festive bazaar. Tickets for workshops via SISTIC. Follow IHC on Facebook. Also see National Heritage Board.

Ticketed Workshops

Indian Heritage Centre has also curated a lineup of ticketed workshops, including hands-on Indian miniature painting and culinary masterclasses featuring North and South Indian biryani styles (Biryani Stories).

 

 

Indian Heritage Centre Deepavali Open House

When: 17 October to 8 November 2026
Where: Indian Heritage Centre
For more information, visit indianheritage.gov.sg
